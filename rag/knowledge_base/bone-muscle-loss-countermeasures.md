## Why bone and muscle are lost

Without gravity, weight-bearing bones and postural muscles are unloaded. Bone resorption increases while formation stays largely unchanged, so calcium and mineral are lost [3]. Leg and back muscles shrink and shift toward faster fiber types, as in other forms of unloading [5].

Key numbers (details in the reference document on body mass, bone and muscle):
- Hip and spine BMD: about 1-1.5% lost per month without effective countermeasures [2].
- Calf muscle volume -13% and peak power -32% after 6 months with the older ISS exercise hardware [5].
- NASA thresholds: hip/spine BMD at least 95% and femoral neck at least 90% of pre-flight (V1 4027) [8]; aerobic capacity and muscle strength at least 80% of pre-flight (V1 4003, V1 4024) [8][1][6].

## Countermeasures that work

- **Heavy resistance exercise**: the Advanced Resistive Exercise Device (ARED, loads up to 600 lbf) replaced an older device limited to about 300 lbf, which had not protected bone [1][3]. With ARED plus adequate energy intake and normal vitamin D status, BMD was maintained in most regions during 4-6 month missions [3].
- **Adequate energy intake**: maintaining body mass was part of the protective combination [3]; vitamin D is supplemented in flight [1].
- **Aerobic exercise** on the T2 treadmill (with harness loading about 68% of body weight) and CEVIS cycle ergometer, prescribed at 70-100% of pre-flight VO2peak or maximum heart rate [6].
- **Medication**: weekly oral alendronate 70 mg plus exercise attenuated nearly all indices of bone loss in 7 ISS astronauts [4]. Bisphosphonates have side effects (GI, rare jaw osteonecrosis) and are not routine; NASA is considering their role for longer missions [1].
- **Exercise without a treadmill (research)**: the HRP Zero T2 study (formal title Exploration Exercise Treadmill Requirements) asks whether removing the treadmill during long-duration flight affects strength, fitness, bone health and balance, because Moon and Mars spacecraft are smaller and treadmills take a lot of space, power and resources [9]. It is an ongoing study; the page gives no results, so the T2 treadmill remains part of ISS practice above.
- Daily training prescription on ISS: about 30 minutes aerobic and 30-60 minutes resistive [6].

## What to do if exercise is missed or painful

1. Missed sessions: report at the next private medical conference; schedule changes are coordinated with the ground strength and conditioning specialists. Exercise intensity, not only volume, matters for muscle [5][6].
2. Pain during exercise: stop the provoking exercise, note location and movement. NASA's shoulder guidance for acute pain (under 4 weeks) is self-care first: activity modification, ice, therapeutic exercises, then acetaminophen/NSAIDs, per the strength and conditioning standing orders; musculoskeletal ultrasound is the initial imaging of choice [7].
3. Back pain: see the back pain document.
4. Crew with venous thrombosis must not use ARED for at least 30 days (see venous thrombosis document).
5. Contact the flight surgeon for acute injury (pop, sudden severe pain, deformity, swelling), joint instability, or numbness/weakness.

## What can and cannot be measured in flight

- BMD is measured by DXA/QCT before and after flight only; there is no in-flight bone density scan [1][2].
- In flight, the proxies are exercise logs (loads, duration, heart rate), body mass trend, nutrition intake and, when collected, urine and blood biomarkers [1][3].
- Hip trabecular bone may not fully recover after flight even when DXA BMD does, so post-flight follow-up continues for years [2].

## Kidney stone link and exploration missions

Calcium released from bone is excreted in urine, raising the risk of calcium kidney stones [1]. ARED plus alendronate also reduced urinary calcium excretion [4]. See the kidney stones document for hydration and symptoms.

Mars-class missions will have smaller, lighter exercise hardware and longer exposure; NASA lists limited exercise equipment as a risk factor for bone loss and kidney stones [1]. Maintaining exercise compliance and energy intake becomes even more important.
