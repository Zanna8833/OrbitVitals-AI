This directory distributes an OrbitVitals-authored factual manifest, not the NASA
scientific files, original ReadMe, complete catalog response, or computed results.

acquisition dates, file sizes and cryptographic digests. No subject observations or
individual descriptive statistics are embedded.

Source: NASA Life Sciences Data Archive (LSDA), NASA Life Sciences Portal (NLSP),
catalog `BRSMACT_Campaign_1_Actigraphy`, experiment `BRSMAct`.
