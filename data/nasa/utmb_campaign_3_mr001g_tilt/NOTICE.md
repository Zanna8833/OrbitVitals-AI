source hashes, byte counts and explicitly limited source-label definitions. It does
not contain original CSV files, full NASA metadata responses, participant-bearing
filenames, private reports, individual observations or participant-level statistics.

**UTMB Bedrest Campaign 3 Tilt**, catalog `MR001G_Campaign_3_Tilt`, experiment
`MR001G`, DOI [10.83263/gz1q-a824](https://doi.org/10.83263/gz1q-a824).
The DOI identifies the experiment, not a newly assigned dataset DOI.

collected in flight. Public download access and original file integrity were
verified. No dataset-specific reuse/redistribution license was found in the
inspected metadata, source files, linked documents or response information.
