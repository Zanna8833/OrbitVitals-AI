## Confirmed global requirements

The [official submission guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/)
permits a video no longer than **30 seconds**, or a deck of at most **seven slides**,
including title/introduction slides. The demo and final-project links must be
publicly accessible without sign-in, password or permission requests.

The deadline is **Sunday, November 15, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. in the registered
Local Event's time zone**. For the Universal Event, the Team Owner's residence time
zone applies. Saving a page is insufficient: the team must submit it for judging
before that deadline. Local judging may have separate arrangements.

The [judging guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/judging-awards-guide/)
requires an official challenge, completed submission fields, confirmed teammates
at one event, English submission/linked materials, and the required attestations.
A non-English video needs English subtitles. Judging covers Impact, Creativity,
Validity, Relevance and Presentation. A NASA resource is sufficient for the stated
minimum resource requirement; that does not guarantee a strong Relevance score or
an eligibility decision.

AI-assisted work must be disclosed accurately. The submission guide specifies
visible indicators for generated images/video and descriptive/metadata disclosure
for generated audio, code and data. Review the exact requirements before release;
see [the disclosure draft](ai-tools-and-credits.md). Generated NASA branding is
not an acceptable media shortcut.
